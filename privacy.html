Privacy Policy

FoodPhotos is built to organize authorized photos without sending photos, analysis results, or location data to iPhoto2.

Summary

FoodPhotos does not require an account.
FoodPhotos does not collect personal data, photos, analysis results, or location data.
Photo analysis runs on the device with Apple Vision and Core ML.

Photo Library access

FoodPhotos accesses only the photos authorized through the iOS Photo Library permission. You can grant full access, limited access, or no access.
The app requests analysis-sized images instead of original-sized images. If an authorized photo is stored in iCloud, Apple PhotoKit may download that photo to the device for local analysis.
FoodPhotos does not upload, copy, edit, cache, or delete original photos.

On-device analysis

FoodPhotos uses Apple Vision to extract a foreground subject before Core ML classifies that subject. It does not fall back to classifying the complete photo.
The app stores local scan records such as an asset identifier, classification result, confidence score, and model version. These records remain on the device and do not contain image data.

FoodPhotos album

When a photo is identified as food, FoodPhotos adds the original asset to a system Photos album named FoodPhotos.
Removing a photo through the app changes only its membership in that album. It does not delete the original asset from the system Photo Library.

Location

With permission, FoodPhotos uses the current device location to center the map. It can also read location metadata from authorized food photos to place them on that map.
Location is not used to classify food. iPhoto2 does not receive or store location data.

Accounts, analytics, and third parties

FoodPhotos has no iPhoto2 backend, account system, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK.
The app uses Apple system frameworks, including PhotoKit, Vision, Core ML, SwiftData, Core Location, and MapKit. Apple services such as iCloud Photos and Maps operate under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

This website

This website does not intentionally use cookies, analytics, accounts, or contact forms.
The website host and content delivery provider may process standard request information, such as an IP address and browser metadata, under their own policies.

Your control

You can change Photo Library and location permissions at any time in iOS Settings.
Deleting the app removes its local app data. The system FoodPhotos album and original photos remain under your control in the Photos app.

Children's privacy

FoodPhotos does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

Policy changes

Material changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@iphoto2.com.
